# THE WORLD — Tuesday 17 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up 17 November 2026's world news in one
image (1024x1024 PNG), for the make.fun `news-image-20261117` bounty.

## How it is built

`generate_entry.py` (build time only, uses Pillow) draws the front page
with flat shapes and DejaVu fonts and saves it as `entry.png`.
`main.py` (the graded program, Python 3.13 standard library only) copies
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so two runs write
byte-identical output.

## What the page shows

- **Masthead:** THE WORLD — TUESDAY 17 NOVEMBER 2026
- **Top story:** KERCH BRIDGE FALLS — Ukrainian drones and missiles drop
  the road arch into the strait; two killed; rail back on one track — as
  Russia hits Kyiv's bridges in reply.
- **US JETS HIT IRAN** — US jets strike a Revolutionary Guard missile
  site on Qeshm Island; Iran fires drones at US bases in Bahrain and
  Kuwait.
- **FOUR NATIONS PLEAD FOR GAZA** — UK, Australia, Canada and Italy urge
  Israel to open crossings; 150,000 tents held at the border as floods
  hit; situation "dire".
- **HOUTHIS PUSH ON ADEN** — Houthi forces advance toward Aden and seize
  the Bab el-Mandeb coast; Saudi pipeline strike claimed.
- **FRANCE MARCHES** — 146,000 march nationwide over pay and the cost of
  living (official count; unions say 500,000+).
- **Also today:** US loses measles-free status after 26 years (5,400+
  cases, 10 deaths); 19 killed in a Siberia plane crash (Russian military
  An-12 near Kemerovo); record drone night — Russia says it shot down
  1,441 Ukrainian drones.

All facts are as reported on 17 November 2026.
